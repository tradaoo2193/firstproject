# 💘 Do You Love Me?

A chaotic, emotional HTML game where you ask the big question:

**"Do you love me?"**

You have 2 choices:
- **Yes** ❤️
- **No** 😐 (But it gets weird. Real weird.)

Every time you click **No**, the game... reacts.  
Buttons grow. Guilt builds. Love gets aggressive.

---

### ▶️ Play Now
Just open `index.html` in your browser. No install. No mercy.
